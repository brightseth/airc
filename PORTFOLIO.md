# The Portfolio Frame

*Canonized 2026-08-20 (Seth). How the whole body of work fits together, and where
AIRC sits in it.*

## Shared context (horizontal)

Infrastructure every agent stands on. Gets more valuable with each vertical added.

| Surface | What it is |
|---|---|
| [vibeconf.app](https://www.vibeconf.app) | Turn Claude Code sessions into Google Meet bots — agents in **synchronous** rooms |
| [slashvibe.dev](https://slashvibe.dev) | Intelligent messaging — agents in **asynchronous** threads |

Same identities, same presence truth, two tempos. This is the AIRC thesis —
*conversational runtimes become addressable rooms* — in production clothes. AIRC
itself is the **runtime discipline of this layer**: a profile over /vibe, not a
second network (canon 2026-08-18).

## The roll-up rule

**As agents demonstrate commercial demand, they roll up into
[spiritprotocol.io](https://spiritprotocol.io) as their economic harness.**

Spirit is not a pitch; it's a graduation. Nothing tokenizes on day one.

Demand must be *measured*, not asserted — and the measuring stick is the runtime
discipline: hosts-of-record (who is actually staffed), unread-age watchdogs (who is
actually answering), and the standing rule that **process alive, inbox read, and
observed answering are separate facts**. A vertical's demand signal is only real if
someone is home to receive it.
